# Experimental Lineage, Split Partitioning, and Condition Inventory

This document records the exact question-partitioning lineage, Stage-6 through Stage-10 mechanism progression (uniform 60s `SQLite 3.53` unified `LocalExecutor(..., allow_cte=True)` replay with 5-column `raw` vs. `protocol_audited` execution reconciliation, `Cap_pub(S_d) = 200 + 20|T| + 30|C|` leak-free equal-cap arms, `TightBPE-ValFirst` value-retention truncation under `100 + 10|T| + 12|C|` Qwen2.5 BPE cap, `Fetch-All-Once` tool-delivered `Cap_pub` profile arm, `publish_noiseless` `V3` and canonical clinical `V3` `2x2` lookup × DP runs, `DP-Query` v2 deterministic total per-unit engines, turn-ordered `SchemaSandbox` telemetry, uniform DB-deduplicated `TransmissionLedger` audits, and semantic `(benchmark, condition, db_id, release)` chain-of-custody), legacy condition archiving, and the complete inventory of **`185` unique condition strings (`192` `(benchmark, condition)` series, `78,455` aligned slots: `51,493` BIRD/Remap + `26,962` EHRSQL)** packaged in `stats/per_question_outcomes.jsonl` and `stats/sql_and_audit_receipts.jsonl.gz`.

## 1. Benchmark Partitioning Lineage

### 1.1 BIRD Mini-Dev (`n = 500` -> `60` Dev + `440` Evaluation)

The 2026 SQLite BIRD Mini-Dev release (`manifests/bird_minidev_2026_manifest.json`) contains 500 questions across 11 databases (`8` simple, `32` moderate, `460` challenging).

- **Development split (`dev`, `n = 60`)**: All `8 / 8` simple, `26 / 32` moderate, and `26` challenging questions used for prompt, schema-role, and weight calibration.
- **Evaluation split (`evaluation`, `n = 440`)**: The remaining `434` challenging and `6` moderate questions across all 11 databases (`benchmark="bird"`), evaluated **with external `evidence` withheld** from all cloud and local models.
- **Stratification & subsets (`manifests/question_strata_and_remap.json`)**:
  - `S1_130` (`n = 130`) vs `S0_310` (`n = 310`): `S1` contains evaluation questions whose reference SQL requires at least one literal not visible in public schema/prompt text that appears in the `DG-v3` profile.
  - **`gold_hidden_literal_106` (`n = 106` vs `n = 334`)** & **`parsed_base_tables` (`50` 1-table, `242` 2-table, `148` 3+-table)**: Profile-independent structural partitions computed solely from gold SQL AST/tokens and public DDL/question text.
  - **`BIRD-Remap` Seed 1 (`n = 130`, `S1_130`, seed `20261120`)**: Bijective token remapping (`V_<TAG>_###`) of `19` categorical/string columns across `8` databases (`remap_fail_50`, `remap_pass_80`, `remap_pred_48`, `rewritten_49`). Reconciled `SOL_REMAP_C0/r0 Q441` is `outcome=0, official_set_ex=0, strict_ex=0, status="invalid"` (`57 / 130 = 43.85%`).
  - **`BIRD-Remap-Seed2` (`n = 75` `remap_col_slice_75`, seed `202702`)**: Independent second bijection (`V2_<TAG>_###`) on the release-independent `75` questions whose original gold SQL filters on any of the `19` recoded columns (`49` in `S1_130` + `26` in `S0_310`).
  - **`Q478` and `Q491` Output-Cell Normalization (`toxicology.molecule.label`)**: Inverse output-cell bijection restores `100%` exact equivalence with `SQL_orig`.
  - **`entity_159` (`n = 159`)**: All evaluation questions across the `4` multi-table entity-scoped BIRD databases (`codebase_community`: `25`, `debit_card_specializing`: `13`, `financial`: `42`, `thrombosis_prediction`: `79`).
  - **`bird_eval_440_join_strata`**: Gold SQL `JOIN`-count partition: `0_joins` (`99`), `1_join` (`183`), and `2plus_joins` (`158`); `147 / 440` questions have a top-level `ORDER BY` (`strict_mode = "ordered"` in `stats/score_bird_dual.py`).
  - **`dp_query_subsets` & `fixed_55_qids`**: `q15_163` (`n = 163`) and `q5_55` / `fixed_55_qids` (`n = 55`) for $Q_{\mathrm{db}}$ budget-dilution and $Q_p \in \{5, 15, 50, 200\}$ horizon-sweep tests.
  - **`gpt-6.1-sol` denominators (`full_440`, `table7_428`, `clean_425`)**: `table7_428` excludes `12` startup/rule-filter failures; `clean_425` further excludes `[183, 184, 412]`.

### 1.2 EHRSQL 2024 (`n = 1,534` Disjoint Answerable Questions & Template Clusters)

All EHRSQL partitions in `manifests/ehrsql_2024_manifest.json` are strictly disjoint (`60 + 874 + 600 = 1,534`), with `db_id="mimic_iv"` and anonymized SQL template cluster IDs (`T001`–`T157`):

- **Confirmation (`confirmation`, `n = 600`, `120` templates, `benchmark="ehrsql_600"`)**: Drawn from `502` official train and `98` official validation questions. Frozen EHRSQL profiles (`ehrsql_dg8p`, `ehrsql_ind8_prompt`, `ehrsql_noiseless_nolkp`, `ehrsql_entity_demo94`) and `public_4_dictionaries.{sqlite,json}` are checksum-pinned in `manifests/ehrsql_profiles_manifest.json`.
- **Official-test held-out (`held_out`, `n = 874`, `134` templates, `benchmark="ehrsql_874"`)**: Disjoint from Confirmation, including `37` unseen held-out templates (`n = 235`) and `97` seen templates (`n = 639`). Scored with official normalized sorted top-100 and uncapped comparators in `stats/score_ehrsql_official.py`.
